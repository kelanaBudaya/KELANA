## Licence observations

- `Attribution only license` Commons category appears on three selected files: `Gapura Masjid Agung Kotagede`, `Gapura Masjid Kagungan Dalem Pajimatan Imogiri`, `Masjid Pajimatan Imogiri`. extmetadata says CC BY-SA 4.0 for these, so they are kept; check the Commons pages before upload. `Masjid Mataram Kotagede.jpg` (same uploader family) has the generic license "Attribution" and was rejected.
- `Gapura Masjid Agung Kotagede`: author text "Fandy Aprianto Rohman" but author_url points to User:Altair_Netraphim (files tagged "Photos by Nerissa Lyra"); identity inconsistent, no multi-author credit.
- Redlink author_urls: Arry gunawan, Rengganis Prajnaparamita.
- `Kota Gede Jogjakarta.jpg` is CC BY-SA 3.0 (also GFDL-tagged, fine).
- Rejected on licence/author: `Sarons and Genders at Pakualaman` (CC BY 2.5, author "No machine-readable author provided"); historical prints with author "Uncredited" or "Unknown".
